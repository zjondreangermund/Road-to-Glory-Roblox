# Road to Glory Roadmap

## V0.1 - Playable football foundation
- Prototype pitch and goals generated from code
- Server-authoritative football actions
- Shoot, pass and dribble controls
- Match clock and score
- Manager rating foundation
- Player/career profile schema
- Original story framework for the Academy Trial
- PC and mobile input foundation

## V0.2 - Academy training
- Sprint drill
- Shooting drill
- Passing drill
- Dribbling drill
- Drill scoring and scout report

## V0.3 - Player creator and progression
- Name, nationality and shirt identity
- Position and preferred foot
- Attributes and archetypes
- XP and training development
- Persistent career saves

## V0.4 - Cinematic story system
- Camera-driven cutscenes
- Dialogue choices
- Manager trust, teammate chemistry, fan and media reputation
- Branching consequences

## V0.5 - Academy Trial chapter
- Arrival cinematic
- Original coach, friend and rival characters
- Full trial sequence
- Trial match
- Scout score
- Three fictional contract offers

## Season 1
Trial -> First Contract -> Debut -> Rival -> First Goal -> Fame -> Bad Form -> Loan/Stay -> Breakthrough -> Cup Final -> International Call-up

## Later systems
Transfers and agents, footballer's lifestyle, international career, Career Online multiplayer, retirement and legacy.
